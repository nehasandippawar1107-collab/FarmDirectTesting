Frequently Asked Questions

How fresh is the produce?

Vegetables and fruits are plucked on the day of harvest, packed at the farm, and dispatched to your door within 24 hours.

How are payments remitted to farmers?

Payments for confirmed orders are directly credited to the farmer's registered bank account or UPI without broker cuts.

What payment methods are supported?

We support Cash on Delivery (COD), UPI (GPay/PhonePe/Paytm), Credit & Debit Cards (Visa/Mastercard/RuPay), and Net Banking.

Can restaurants place bulk orders?

Yes! Cafés and restaurants can contact us directly via the form above or phone to schedule recurring farm crate dispatches.
